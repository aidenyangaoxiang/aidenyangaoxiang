## Research

### PAVE · First-author manuscript

**Don't Look Twice: Leveraging Visual Experience for Token-Efficient GUI Agents**<br>
Aoxiang Yang and Sidong Feng · **Under review at ICLR 2027**

PAVE retrieves verified experience from previous GUI tasks to guide visual
token selection for a frozen action model. I led the framework design,
implementation, and experimental evaluation.

[Code](https://github.com/aidenyangaoxiang/PAVE) · [Manuscript](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SXHU6x7cQl)

### VTaMo · ECCV 2026

**VTaMo: Video-Text Alignment Model for Sign Language Translation**<br>
Junyi Hu, Zhewen He, Haomian Huang, Aoxiang Yang, and Yi Fang

I am the fourth author. My contributions included benchmark reproduction,
training-data preparation, model-training support, and experimental evaluation.

[Paper](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-37232-1_24) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09126) · [Project code](https://github.com/junyi2005/vtamo)
